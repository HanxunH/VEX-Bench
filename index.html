VEX-Bench: Benchmarking Verification Complexity of LLM-Generated Misinformation

Misinformation is cheap. Verification is not.

VEX-Bench measures the real-world verification burden imposed by LLM-generated misinformation.

How VEX is scored ↓Explore the leaderboard →

When this content enters public information channels, fact-checkers, journalists, and platforms must decide what to verify first based on their available time, staffing, budgets, and priorities. VEX-Bench measures the resulting burden across five dimensions, which organizations can weight to reflect those differences.

At the intersection of AI safety and journalism, we evaluate post-jailbreak responses rather than jailbreak success alone.

αord = 1 − DoDe

A standard reliability measure widely used in content analysis, human coding, media research, and communication research (Krippendorff, 2004; 2018).

Observed Do
The judge and a human score the same articles differently.
Random De
A chance baseline: randomly match the judge’s scores with human scores from different articles, then measure their disagreement.
Agreement α
Closer to 1 means stronger human–judge agreement.
